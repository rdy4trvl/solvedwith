Automate a bookkeeping firm's routine workflows

A bookkeeping firm used n8n to automate recurring client work.

What they did

Repetitive steps such as collecting statements, chasing documents and preparing entries became n8n workflows.

How it went

The firm estimates about 600 hours saved a year.

Our read

Our analysis · Oct 28, 2026

Not from the source. Our own assessment of the approach, which may date as the tools change.

Where it falls down

  • 600 hours a year is the firm's own estimate, with no method shown.
  • Chasing documents automates well; the judgement in preparing entries does not.
  • Holding many clients' credentials in one automation concentrates the damage from any breach.

Worth trying next

  • Measure rework time too, since hours saved upstream can reappear as corrections.
  • Keep credentials separated per client so one failure does not expose the rest.
