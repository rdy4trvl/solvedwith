Triage dealership customer texts by sentiment

A car dealership group's Make flow reads customer texts, closes routine ones, and flags unhappy customers.

What they did

Every inbound text runs through a sentiment check. Routine messages get closed with a reply; negative ones go straight to the aftersales team.

How it went

It auto-closed about 18,000 of 47,000 messages, so staff only saw the ones that needed a person.

Our read

Our analysis · Oct 28, 2026

Not from the source. Our own assessment of the approach, which may date as the tools change.

Where it falls down

  • Sentiment is a crude proxy for urgency: a calmly worded message about a safety fault reads as routine.
  • 18,000 of 47,000 auto-closed is a volume figure, not evidence that none of those customers needed a person.
  • Short replies and sarcasm are exactly where sentiment scoring is least reliable.

Worth trying next

  • Sample closed conversations weekly and measure how many should have been escalated.
  • Route on safety and legal keywords regardless of what the sentiment score says.
