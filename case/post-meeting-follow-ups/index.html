Handle follow-ups after every meeting

An agent finds the transcript, pulls out action items, messages teammates, and books the next meeting.

What they did

After a meeting ends, the agent reads the transcript, lists action items, posts them to Slack, and checks calendars to schedule a follow-up.

How it went

Shown as a vendor demo, so no real-world results are reported.

Our read

Our analysis · Oct 28, 2026

Not from the source. Our own assessment of the approach, which may date as the tools change.

Where it falls down

  • This is a vendor demo with no real results, so the accuracy of the action items is unknown.
  • Transcripts miss what was agreed with a nod, and tend to invent structure that was not there.
  • It posts to Slack and schedules with no approval, so a wrong action item becomes a commitment.

Worth trying next

  • Post the action items to the organiser as a draft first.
  • Measure how many items get edited or deleted after posting.
