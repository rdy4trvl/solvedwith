Marketing & agenciesWorked

Create and edit ad campaigns by talking to an agent

Marketers pull normalised ad data and make campaign changes in conversation, with every new campaign created paused until someone approves it.

What they did

A marketer asks a question, the agent calls the reporting tools and gets back normalised data carrying its source, timestamp and transformation logic, then recommends what to do next. Write actions cover Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok, LinkedIn and Snapchat. New campaigns are created paused rather than live, and an approval page lists each change for a stakeholder to accept or reject.

How it went

Active users of the connector grew 250% month over month on average since a February launch. A 25-person agency cut a 10-hour client report to 20 minutes, 17 of which went on pasting the finished data into the client's spreadsheet, and found no incorrect data points across a multi-week validation against live campaigns. The figures are the vendor's and that agency's own.

Where it falls down

  • “No incorrect data points” came from one 25-person agency checking its own work, not an independent audit, and errors nobody noticed are not errors that did not happen.
  • Paused-by-default and the approval page are what make the write actions safe. The version that monitors and acts on its own is described only as planned.
  • 250% month-over-month growth measures how many people started using it, not whether the campaigns did better.

Worth trying next

  • Keep campaigns paused by default even after you trust it. The cost is one click; the downside is live spend.
  • Compare performance against campaigns you built by hand, since adoption and results are separate questions.
