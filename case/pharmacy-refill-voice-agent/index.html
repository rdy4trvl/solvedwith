Healthcare, clinics & dentalWorked

Handle pharmacy refill calls with a voice agent

A pharmacy's phone agent identifies callers, submits refills, and routes renewal requests to prescribers.

What they did

The pharmacy put a voice agent on its refill line. It confirms who is calling, checks the prescription, submits the refill, or queues a renewal request for the prescriber.

How it went

It handled about 380 refills in its first 30 days. Calls it can't resolve go to staff.

Our read

Our analysis · Oct 28, 2026

Not from the source. Our own assessment of the approach, which may date as the tools change.

Where it falls down

  • Identifying a caller by voice is the weak point: accents, background noise and bad lines all raise the chance of matching the wrong patient record.
  • 380 refills is self-reported with no error rate beside it, so there is no way to tell how many were wrong.
  • No approval step is described, so a misheard drug or dose reaches the prescriber queue unchecked.

Worth trying next

  • Require a second identifier, such as date of birth, before any refill is submitted.
  • Log every call where the match was low-confidence and review those weekly rather than sampling at random.
