Manufacturing & supply chainWorked

Find and parse chemical safety data sheets

An app built with Manus finds the right SDS PDF online, downloads it, and turns it into structured data.

What they did

Given a product name, the system searches for the safety data sheet, downloads the PDF, extracts the text and maps it to a fixed schema.

How it went

The author built almost the whole app through Manus.

Our read

Our analysis · Oct 28, 2026

Not from the source. Our own assessment of the approach, which may date as the tools change.

Where it falls down

  • The right-looking sheet may be the wrong revision, and safety data changes between versions.
  • PDF extraction is uneven, and the sections that matter most are often the worst formatted.
  • There is no approval step on data people may rely on when handling chemicals.

Worth trying next

  • Capture the revision date and source URL with every sheet, and re-check on a schedule.
  • Validate the parsed schema against a set of known-good sheets before trusting it.
