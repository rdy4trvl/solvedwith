Retail & e-commerceFailed

Handle store customer support with an AI agent

A merchant ran Gorgias's AI agent for months, then switched it off.

What they did

The agent answered customer emails and chats for an online store.

How it went

It gave irrelevant answers and promised things the store couldn't deliver, so the merchant disabled it.

Our read

Our analysis · Oct 28, 2026

Not from the source. Our own assessment of the approach, which may date as the tools change.

Where it falls down

  • Promising what the store could not deliver is worse than no answer: it creates obligations.
  • Failures like this usually trace to missing grounding rather than a weak model.
  • Sending to customers with no approval step means mistakes land before anyone sees them.

Worth trying next

  • Ground every answer in the policy documents and refuse anything they do not cover.
  • Start in draft mode and enable sending only for question types with a clean record.
